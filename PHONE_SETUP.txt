PUTTING GOOS ON AN ANDROID PHONE (view only)

ONE TIME - publish the app (about 10 minutes)
 1. Go to github.com and sign in (or create a free account).
 2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name it goos-phone.
    Choose Public. Click Create repository.
 3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in EVERY file from this
    folder (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, the icons and logo.png
    if there is one).
    Click Commit changes.
 4. Settings > Pages. Under Branch choose "main" and "/ (root)". Save.
 5. Wait 1-2 minutes. The page shows the address, like
    https://YOURNAME.github.io/goos-phone/

ON EACH PHONE
 1. Open that address in Chrome.
 2. Sign in with the person's normal GOOS username and password.
 3. Chrome menu (three dots) > Add to Home screen > Install.

WHAT EACH PERSON SEES
 Only the GOOS tabs they are allowed on the laptop. The CEO sees all.
 Five wrong passwords lock that user out of the phone for 15 minutes.
 Changing a password in GOOS signs that person out of every phone.

WHEN GOOS IS UPDATED
 Run Phone App again in GOOS and upload the new files the same way
 (Add file > Upload files, replace). Nothing to do on the phones.

The address is public but shows nothing without a GOOS sign-in.
